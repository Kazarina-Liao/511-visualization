Key Insights

96% of encampments are within a 20-min walk of food — yet only 49% are near a shelter, and 54% near transit.

Hot spots in District 6 (NW) and District 3 (Capitol Hill/CD) have the highest report density but uneven shelter coverage.

Reports have grown 6× since 2018, peaking in 2025 with 76,790 reports — underscoring rising demand for all three resource types.
